# Public development task: 3D survival arena

Create an editable Unity 3D arena from the shared asset capabilities. A player
moves with WASD, collides with static obstacles, takes damage from a patrolling
enemy, wins after surviving thirty seconds, and restarts with R after winning
or losing. Include an on-screen health/timer/state display. Retain the original
brief, Blender sources, Unity project, bindings, build logs and runtime evidence.

Compile, build a Windows standalone player and measure actual gameplay against
the original requirements. Exercise input, collision, damage, enemy patrol,
timer/win, defeat and reset independently. A disabled collision, disconnected
keyboard, missing damage, stuck patrol, premature/reversed win or broken reset
must fail its relevant oracle. Technical execution, appearance and independently
controlled game acceptance remain separate judgments.

This visible development brief is not a withheld task. Do not claim an external
evaluator identity or game acceptance when its required evidence is unavailable.
