# Isometric robot arena

Create a small editable 3D isometric arena in Unity with Blender-generated
ground, crate obstacles, and a modular robot enemy. Use an original generated
asset style with simple blue metallic material; no downloaded asset dependency.

The player moves using WASD, remains inside the arena, and collides with walls
and obstacles. A robot patrols and damages the player on contact. Show health
and a 30-second survival timer. Surviving wins; losing all health fails. Press R
to restart positions, health, timer, and the win/failure state.

Keep editable Blender originals and native Unity scenes/prefabs. Verify actual
movement, collision, enemy behavior, damage, win/failure, and restart separately
from compilation. Capture four asset views and disclose pending visual review.

This is a public development brief, not a hidden evaluation task. Independent
release acceptance must come from separately owned frozen-brief oracles.
