usage: polyforge review [-h] {sprite-source,amend,board,human} ...

positional arguments:
  {sprite-source,amend,board,human}
    sprite-source       Inspect and seal sprite sources before native import
    amend               Append explicit original-bound operator obligations
    board               Read-only technical, subjective and gameplay promotion
                        review
    human               Record an explicit local operator decision for a
                        sealed revision
